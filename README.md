# Youth Coach Behavioral Assessment (YCBA)

A research prototype for a structured behavioral screen designed to flag risk markers in youth-sport coaching **before licensure** — part of an athlete-safeguarding initiative.

> ⚠️ **Prototype — not a validated instrument.** Items are adapted from published descriptions of established measures but have **not** been psychometrically validated in this combination. It must not be used for any real hiring, licensure, disciplinary, or clinical decision.

## The instrument (YCBA-SR v1.0)

A 39-item self-report form across five scales, each with named facets grounded in the research literature:

- **Scale A — Grandiose Narcissism** — entitlement, exploitativeness, lack of empathy, need for admiration, arrogance *(adapted from the Five-Factor Narcissism Inventory, FFNI)*
- **Scale B — Callous / Antisocial Tendencies** — callousness, deceit, rule-disregard, impulsivity *(Levenson Self-Report Psychopathy Scale, LSRP)*
- **Scale C — Anger & Aggression** — temper, verbal aggression, physical aggression, hostility *(Buss–Perry Aggression Questionnaire; STAXI)*
- **Scale D — Emotional Volatility & Controlling Attachment** — affective instability, idealization–devaluation, reactivity, conditional regard *(McLean Screening Instrument for BPD; Controlling Coach Behaviours Scale)*
- **Scale V — Response Validity** — impression-management check for a "fake-good" response style *(Balanced Inventory of Desirable Responding, BIDR)*

Scoring computes facet- and scale-level means (1–5), banded Low / Moderate / Elevated, plus a validity index. Results are framed to **route attention to a trained reviewer** — never to decide an outcome on their own.

## Usage

Open `YCBA_Assessment_Mockup.html` in any modern web browser. Everything runs locally; no data is transmitted or stored.
