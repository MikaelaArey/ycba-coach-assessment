Research Prototype
Prototype — not a validated instrument. This form demonstrates the structure and item logic of the YCBA concept. Items are adapted from the research literature on established measures (FFNI, LSRP, Buss–Perry, MSI-BPD, Controlling Coach Behaviours Scale) but have not been psychometrically validated in this combination. It must not be used for any real hiring, licensure, disciplinary, or clinical decision.
Confidentiality. In a live deployment, responses would be stored securely, released only to trained reviewers, and governed by informed consent under an approved IRB protocol. This demo stores nothing and runs entirely in your browser.

Instructions

The statements below describe thoughts, feelings, and behaviors a coach might have. For each one, indicate how characteristic or true it is of you as a coach, using the 5-point scale. There are no right or wrong answers — the screen works only when responses are candid.

Answer every item. Rate your typical self across a full season, not a single good or bad day.
